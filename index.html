Sun LifeAssure

Sun LifeAssure is a non-participating, 5-year renewable term health insurance plan that provides financial protection against any of the covered critical illness conditions or untimely passing, whichever comes first. It offers coverage until age 70 with premiums increasing every 5 years based on attained age.
